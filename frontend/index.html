Smart Route & ETA Engine

Interactive routing demo on real OpenStreetMap data for Bhubaneswar, India

1 Pick two points

Click the map twice: first click = start, second click = end. Every button below (sections 2-4) fires a fresh computation for whatever pair you picked, via the dev server's /compute or /compute_k endpoints - see scripts/dev_server.py. Tip: pick points a few km apart for a snappy demo - Dijkstra animates one marker per node expanded, and very distant pairs can expand thousands of nodes.

2 Single route

Computes the A*/distance route for the two points above.

3 Search visualization demo

Runs Dijkstra, A*, and bidirectional Dijkstra on the same two points and animates all three searches at the same time, side by side: Dijkstra   A*   Bidirectional Dijkstra - watch bidirectional Dijkstra's wave grow from both ends at once and meet in the middle, instead of spreading from just one point.

4 K-shortest validated routes

Runs Yen's algorithm for the top K distinct routes between the two points above, then discards any that fail the validation chain (closed roads / sanity bounds / reachability) - see NOTES.md.

5 Live road conditions

Turn on edit mode, then click near a road to mark it. The change applies immediately - no server restart, no re-export. Marked roads are sent to the engine on every subsequent route computation.

6 Route cache

Results are cached in the dev server, keyed by (start node, end node, algo, weight, k). Marking a road evicts only the cached routes that actually drive down it.
